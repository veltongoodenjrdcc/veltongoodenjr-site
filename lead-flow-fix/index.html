The leak math

A service business missing two warm leads a week could be leaking serious money without noticing.

These are illustrative examples, not guaranteed losses. The point is simpler: warm interest has value, and messy handoffs quietly waste it.

2 missed leads/week

Barber or salon

The problem you already know

Getting messages is one thing. Turning them into business is another.

This is not a traffic-generation promise. Lead Flow Fix cleans up the path after someone has already shown interest.

  • WhatsApp messages get buried under everything else happening that day.
  • Instagram and Facebook DMs get answered late, or not in the same tone twice.
  • Customers ask for price, location, hours, booking steps, then disappear.
  • The same questions get answered manually every day.
  • The owner is the only person who knows what should happen next.
  • Follow-up depends on memory, which means some warm leads quietly cool off.

The 14-day build

How the work moves

The point is not to bury you in tools. The point is to leave your business with a clearer way to handle warm interest after someone reaches out.

"14-day build" refers to the build period. Aftercare extends beyond the build so the setup has room to settle into real use.

Day 1-2

Leak Audit

Map where warm messages arrive, where they stall, and what must be cleaned up first.

Day 3-5

WhatsApp Business setup or cleanup

Tighten profile details, labels, quick replies, and the main action path.

Day 6-7

Single source of truth Lead Sheet

Build the lightweight tracker for stages, owners, follow-up dates, and status.

Day 8-9

DM-to-WhatsApp bridge

Align social bios, bridge messages, Google touchpoints, and links.

Day 10-11

Ghosted-lead reactivation flow

Write the 5-touch sequence, including a respectful close-the-loop message.

Day 12

Handover training

Walk the owner or responder through the system, record the session, and leave the cheat sheet.

Day 13-26

Aftercare

Light support after handover so the system is easier to keep using.

FAQ

Fair questions before you book

What exactly is Lead Flow Fix?

A 14-day build that cleans up how warm leads move from messages to next steps. It gives you one front door, one tracker, reusable replies, and a follow-up flow.

Do we need new software?

No new software by default. The build uses Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business, and simple tracking unless there is a clear reason to work around an existing tool.

Is this social media management?

No. It may clean up social CTAs and bridge messages, but it does not include posting calendars, community management, or content production.

Is this paid ads?

No. Lead Flow Fix does not promise more traffic. It improves what happens after people already reach out.

What if we already have a CRM?

If it genuinely works, we work around it. If it is part of the mess, the build focuses on the simplest reliable path first.

Do you take control of our accounts?

No. Access is handled only where needed for setup, review, or handover. The business keeps ownership of its accounts.

What happens if staff do not use it after handover?

The handover, recording, cheat sheet, and aftercare reduce that risk. Still, the owner or manager has to support the new way of handling messages.

What if we cancel before kickoff?

Pre-kickoff cancellation by the client receives a deposit refund minus a J$10,000 admin fee. Other refund scenarios are handled in writing against the agreed terms.
